Compliance built in

Personal data is handled in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles, with consent captured at every collection point. Security is aligned to recognised international standards with penetration testing and a shareable summary. Course video is streamed through protected, expiring links.

03

Website enhancements.

From the Website Enhancement Brief and the 7 August meeting. Section A items are mandatory and lead the build. Section B items follow. Design direction is executed to a high standard using the Calcite creative license, benchmarked against the consultancy sites you admire, with no pixel level references required.

Section A, mandatory

ItemWhat we build
A1 Homepage overhaulA single page homepage. Visitors move through it using a top ribbon navigation rather than long scrolling. Priority is smooth execution, sharp image resolution and readability. Video led first view, benchmarked against McKinsey, Deloitte, PwC, EY, Bain and FTI Consulting.
A2 First load animationA brief, refined loading animation, a small animated logo that appears and resolves into the homepage. Strong first impression that still reads as enterprise, smooth with no jump or flicker, consistent across browsers.
A3 Fade transitionsSmooth fade transitions when switching the Win and Award toggle and when using dropdowns and slicers. Consistent timing site wide, no jarring cuts, no left to right movement, never blurry.
A4 Multi shaded backgroundBackgrounds that blend multiple shades of green and black with visible depth and variation, replacing the current flat single tone. Palette agreed with you before prototyping, all text kept accessible over it.
A5 Service Offering pageCapabilities renamed to Service Offering, with the Win Contracts and Award Contracts offerings made clearly and structurally different, not just relabelled. Six services listed. Presented with animation. Concept shared for your approval, content supplied by you. Deferred to the next wave by agreement.
A6 Procurement Advisor toolA self contained, rules based lookup that gives users a procurement approach for their jurisdiction, starting with New South Wales. No AI engine, no data storage, logic only. Built so new rule files can be swapped in as regulations change, with no rebuild. Disclaimer always visible.

Section B, secondary

ItemWhat we build
B1 Articles progress barA reading progress bar at the top of article pages that tracks scroll position, with the title fixed at the top and share, print and download options.
B2 Government panelsA new page listing current panels and prequalification schemes, organised by jurisdiction and easy to scan, updatable by you where feasible. Modelled on a clean list format.
B3 Tender website linksA Local Government section added between the Federal and State sections. The Login Required indicator corrected to show only for South Australia, removed elsewhere.
B4 AI Prompt LibraryA new page of master prompts and use cases, structured as Main Topic (Win, Award, Other) then Use Case then AI tool. Limited to ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. Laid out on the Courses page structure with easy copy, avoiding the result display style you disliked.
B5 Policies rewritePage structure and design ready to receive your rewritten Privacy, Terms and Conflict of Interest content when supplied.
B6 Templates pageA new Templates section on the top ribbon offering downloadable documents (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), browsable with a slicer to organise them, similar to the Prompt Library. Sourced and licence checked before publishing.
B7 Find a Bid WriterAn advertising placement for bid management companies, with filters for office location and category. Built and tested but held off the live site until you confirm go live, once companies are signed up to a placement fee.
04

Release plan.

To hold the 31 August date with confidence, scope is split into a core release that lands on the date and a short fast follow for the items that depend on your content or benefit from a second iteration.

2.1 Core, by 31 August

Everything a learner and your team needs day one, plus the mandatory website items.

  • Full learning flow, payments with GST, certificates, admin
  • Homepage, palette, loader, fade transitions, advisor tool (NSW)
  • Articles bar, panels, tender links, prompt library, templates
  • Find a Bid Writer built and held
2.2 Fast follow, September

Items that move with your content or gain from a second pass.

  • Service Offering page rebuild (with your copy)
  • Policies pages (with your content)
  • Advisor tool, additional jurisdictions
  • Affiliate program, live sessions, course coach, deeper CRM and gamification
05

Out of scope and assumptions.

  • Content is supplied by you. Course lessons and videos, Service Offering copy, policy content, the New South Wales rule file, and the source template documents are provided by your side. We build the structure and load the content.
  • Advisor logic is swap in only. We integrate and display your rule files. We do not author or test the underlying jurisdictional logic.
  • Find a Bid Writer stays off the live site until you instruct go live.
  • Trading entity for GST (Australia or United Arab Emirates) is confirmed with you before payment go live.
  • Template sourcing and licensing is confirmed before any template is published.

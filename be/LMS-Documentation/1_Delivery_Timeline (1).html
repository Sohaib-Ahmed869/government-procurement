03

Feedback days.

The working model we agreed on 7 August: we present, you give quick and direct feedback, and we keep the loop tight so the process never gets overwhelming. Feedback given by the end of a Friday is actioned first thing the following Monday.

  • Friday 15 August, Checkpoint 1. Review the new homepage direction, the shortlisted colour palettes, and the Procurement Advisor tool. Your approval on palette and homepage direction unlocks the full visual build.
  • Friday 22 August, Checkpoint 2. Walk through the full learning flow (enrol, watch, complete, certificate) and the new website pages. Flag anything to adjust before hardening.
  • Friday 29 August, Checkpoint 3. Final staging walkthrough and user acceptance. We capture the last change list here so launch day is presentation only.
  • Monday 31 August, Presentation and go live. Final walkthrough of the delivered platform, then we push everything live on your domain.
One turnaround rule

We reply to any message within one business day and confirm anything material in writing the same day. Feedback that changes scope is agreed openly and never quietly absorbed into the timeline.

Why Enrollment sits in the middle

Everything gated behind a purchase reads from one record. "Can this person watch this video" is a single lookup on { student, course }, which is also the unique index, so a learner can never end up with two enrolments in the same course.

02

The entities.

Five collections. Two already exist and are reused as-is; three are new for the learning platform.

EntityStatusWhat it holdsKey rule
UserExisting One account for a website visitor and a student, single sign-on. New student role, in no privileged group.
CourseExisting The sellable product: title, price, availability, marketing copy. Stays the catalogue record, no learner data on it.
LessonNew One step of a course: video, text, PDF or quiz, grouped into modules. slug unique per course, not globally.
EnrollmentNew A learner's access to a course, payment snapshot, progress and quiz scores. One per { student, course } pair.
CertificateNew The issued, publicly verifiable completion record with its PDF. Immutable once issued, revoked rather than deleted.
